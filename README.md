I am a **CSE Student** dedicated to building high-performance applications and exploring the cutting edge of technology. Currently, I'm deep-diving into **Angular.js** and **Kubernetes** to strengthen my full-stack and DevOps capabilities.

- 🔭 **Currently working on:**
